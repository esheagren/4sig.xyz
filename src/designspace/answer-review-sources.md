# Answer-review editorial notes

Checked 2026-09-12. These are Design Space drafts, not edits to the live question bank.

Use plain sentences for substantial ideas. The first sentence should add a trend, a meaningful contrast, or a surprising fact. The two expanded sentences should add evidence and texture. Avoid generic lessons about why knowledge matters, obvious policy implications, or telling the reader what to value. Keep dates, denominators, geography, and data editions clear; distinguish percentage changes from percentage-point changes. Each draft carries its own context links alongside the existing answer source.

## Verification

- **Median age:** [UN WPP 2024, distributed by Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/median-age?tab=table&time=1970..2020&country=OWID_WRL~CHN~JPN~NER). Downloaded chart CSV and metadata: world 20.308 (1970) → 29.575 (2020), a 9.267-year increase; China 18.026 → 37.494, just over double; Japan 47.674 and Niger 14.821 in 2020. Rounded in copy. **Question-bank follow-up:** the existing scored answer of 31 uses an older estimate; WPP 2024 gives 29.575. Do not present a trend assembled from these different editions. This copy uses WPP 2024 throughout; the question/scoring benchmark is unchanged in this design-only edit.
- **Urbanization:** [UN's 2018 release](https://www.un.org/development/desa/en/news/population/2018-revision-of-world-urbanization-prospects.html) gives 55% in 2018, 751 million urban residents in 1950 and 4.2 billion in 2018, Latin America and Caribbean 81%, Africa 43%. The matching 2018 report gives 30% for 1950. All figures use that edition and national urban definitions; do not mix these with the newer harmonized cities/towns classification.
- **Agricultural employment:** [World Bank API, ILO modelled estimates, indicator SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/BDI;USA/indicator/SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS?date=2022&format=json), retrieved values Burundi 85.6818575535412%, United States 1.62054352999574% for 2022. This measure includes agriculture, forestry, and fishing. [FAO's 2024 yearbook release](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-statistical-yearbook-2024-reveals-critical-insights-on-the-sustainability-of-agriculture-food-security-and-the-importance-of-agrifood-in-employment/) gives global employment shares 40% → 26% and real agricultural value growth of 89% over 2000–2022. A falling employment share does not imply fewer workers in absolute terms.
- **Shipping:** [UNCTAD RMT 2024](https://unctad.org/publication/review-maritime-transport-2024) reports 2023 cargo volume growth 2.4%, ton-mile growth 4.2%, and longer distances from disruption at Suez/Panama. Copy explains ton-miles as weight multiplied by distance; it is not an increase in cargo weight alone.
- **Nuclear:** [IAEA 2024 status poster](https://pris.iaea.org/PRIS/PRIS_poster_2024.pdf): France 67.3%, China 4.5%; nuclear electricity 364.4 and 417.5 TWh respectively; China 28 reactors under construction at year end. 1 TWh = 1 billion kWh. This compares shares of national electricity with absolute nuclear generation.
- **Fresh water:** [USGS water stores](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/freshwater-lakes-and-rivers-and-water-cycle): groundwater 30.1% of freshwater; freshwater lakes 0.26%, rivers 0.006%; Lake Baikal about 20% of fresh surface water. Rounded lake-plus-river share to 0.3%. Surface water and all freshwater are different denominators.
- **Sequencing:** [NHGRI timeline](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/fact-sheets/Sequencing-Human-Genome-cost): about $14 million in 2006, below $1,500 in late 2015. [NHGRI methods](https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/fact-sheets/DNA-Sequencing-Costs-Data): the sharp fall begins in January 2008 with the switch to second-generation sequencing. These are production-cost benchmarks, not a patient's retail bill or the total Human Genome Project budget.
- **Parliament:** [IPU historical review](https://www.ipu.org/resources/publications/reports/2020-03/women-in-parliament-1995-2020-25-years-in-review) gives 11.3% in 1995; [2026 release](https://www.ipu.org/news/press-releases/2026-03/womens-representation-in-parliament-sees-sluggish-gains) gives 27.5% on January 1, 2026 and +0.3 percentage points during 2025; [2026 map](https://www.ipu.org/file/23158/download) ranks Rwanda's lower house first at 63.8%. Global figure covers parliamentary seats; the country ranking uses lower/single houses.
